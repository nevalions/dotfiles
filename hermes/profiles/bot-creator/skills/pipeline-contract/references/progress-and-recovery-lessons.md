## Progress and completion discipline

For **every future Kanban workflow**, load [outcome-first review-loop guard](review-loop-guard.md) at admission and before correction/review handoff. Orchestrator must invoke `python scripts/review_loop_guard.py sanitized-evidence.json` on retained full stage history; this is caller-enforced policy, not a native dispatcher hook. Default two rejections per authorized cycle; reserve one bounded correction and independent re-review for a concrete first finding within the approved implementation budget, rather than making every ordinary review defect a new operator decision. Stop earlier for explicit no-correction scope, exhausted authority, or the same severe finding after correction; never retroactively override those restrictions. Review-routing recovery is not an author correction. Use role/task-sized cumulative author+review budgets, meaningful no-progress checkpoints, bounded architecture escalation and exact unchanged-model decisions. Keep optional supporting work off the causal critical path, reuse immutable evidence/diffs/context, run targeted iteration tests then full required candidate gates, and never treat unknown usage as zero or provider percentage as task tokens. Stop/park rather than approve or launch more supporting cards. Preserve all independent review and production/publication gates. For a separately authorized diagnostic redesign, use [structured diagnostic replacement](structured-diagnostics.md); it is an optional design contract, not automatic priority or permission to resume parked work.

Before restarting a timed-out planning or research task, inspect durable artifact completeness and write evidence, and separately verify its owner session is idle. A complete deliverable may exist even when the terminal review handoff timed out. Preserve the artifact and failed-run provenance; recover review through supported native transitions when valid, or use an explicitly scoped independent review card with correct dependency wiring and ordinary-card lifecycle semantics. Never fabricate author completion or review provenance, and do not repeat full planning solely because the board says blocked. For a verified timed-out planner's complete preserved artifact, use only the explicit [recovered plan review schema](recovered-plan-review.md): independent ordinary-card approval, exact artifact hashes and candidate bindings, verified source idle, and an Orchestrator decision linked to actual user workflow-recovery authorization. Keep `native=false`; this is not a generic review bypass. Keep final incident acceptance blocked until its actual criteria pass. A dispatcher timeout is not proof that the gateway-owned model has stopped.

Choose budgets by role and required evidence, not by copying the research cap everywhere. A short artifact-only triage may use 12 tool invocations; multi-repository implementation planning needs room for mandatory skills, repository instructions, candidate identity, diagnosis, source reads, output and review handoff. Do not treat a larger tool-call allowance as a fix for an undersized wall-clock budget: required reads, model latency, artifact writing and terminal handoff all consume runtime. Budget those phases separately, persist the deliverable incrementally, and reserve time for the native transition rather than waiting until the final response to save work. Size planner, reviewer, implementer, tester and deployment budgets separately against their required evidence and repository breadth. Record role, runtime, tool-call ceiling, checkpoint interval, wrap-up reserve and stop conditions at admission; verify the native runtime limit before dispatch. Keep research-specific ceilings confined to research. Require evidence-first exact-file reads. These call ceilings are procedural, not native enforcement. Preserve partial artifacts and diagnose denied commands without retrying or bypassing guards. Exhaustion caused by an undersized admission budget is an orchestration issue, not missing human input; explicitly authorize at most one corrected continuation rather than silently extending or looping. Never extend production/publication authority when correcting a planning budget.
Load [bounded research](bounded-research.md) before research/diagnostic admission. Size each actual stage within the user task scope; five-minute triage is a fallback for unscoped research, not a universal incident cap or a reason to seek approval for every read. Record task-scoped diagnostic budgets and automatically continue already-authorized phases. Run the `research` validator for diagnostic admission and relevant development gates for repository changes. Native start/phase checkpoints, evidence reuse, terminal handoff and distinct final acceptance are mandatory.

For declarative settings synchronization, supply one source-to-destination manifest before admission: exact owned paths/keys, preserved settings, secret exclusions and expected consumer behavior. Group native edits by destination file and reuse unchanged source evidence; repeated rediscovery is not independent verification. Apply schema, effective-config, secret-exclusion and affected consumer checks to settings-only deltas; add full relevant installer regressions when installer behavior changes. Require an early saved candidate and a checkpoint stating mapped/changed/tested/remaining, with a receipt-writing reserve. Keep independent review and destination-drift checks; do not split each checklist item into another routing card.

Keep the full stage ledger and rejection policy coordinator-owned and append-only; authors supply evidence references, not replacement guard histories or altered retry limits. Add current amendment consumption without erasing historical STOP outcomes.

When repairing a maintained recovery gate, test the real intended admission packet and its full author-plus-review budget before activation, not only convenient short synthetic examples. Compare the independently reviewed ceiling with the already-authorized plan; an isolated suite can pass while the actual workflow remains inadmissible. Preserve legacy defaults unless separately supplied owner authority explicitly selects a different finite bound. Test exact expiry, absent/invalid bounds, candidate/history mismatch and correction admission followed by fresh-test independent acceptance. Admission may permit creating missing evidence; it must never mark historical missing tests PASS or equate correction admission with final approval. Verify deployed bytes against the reviewed snapshot and run the focused consumer regression before coordinator handoff. Correct an implementation mismatch within the approved repair instead of repeatedly asking for the same generic retry approval; a genuinely larger scope or authority window still requires a new explicit decision.

Before accepting a recovery manifest, compare the complete retained stage ledger against its input and verify base commit, HEAD, index/staged/unstaged identity, owned-path scope, file types/modes/link targets/deletions and deterministic digest. Include repository/base/HEAD/scope and separate index/worktree records inside the canonical hashed payload, not merely its printed header. Reject every nonignored changed path outside the explicit scope; proving only that listed paths exist is insufficient. Test independent changes to base, HEAD, staged bytes and scope while retaining final worktree bytes; each identity change must change the digest or fail validation. Bind fresh test receipts to that identity without relabeling historical results. Verify the exact test-script digest against the retained successful invocation; unexplained script changes invalidate reused results. Treat a recovered harness that deletes its own evidence root as evidence-only, not a reproducible test recipe. Run dry-run regressions through the real entrypoint and assert no filesystem changes, including backup directories, because a helper may write before its dry-run branch.

For file-identity and exclusion helpers, validate the entire path domain before accepting another corrective handoff:

1. Parse Git's machine-readable NUL-delimited records by their documented status format. Preserve literal filename bytes; never split a non-rename filename on human-display delimiters such as ` -> `, because that can silently bind a different path. Test discovery through a disposable real Git repository as well as unit fixtures; mocked output alone may hide parser assumptions.
2. Enumerate individual nonignored files under untracked directories rather than hashing a directory placeholder. Require nested edits and additions to change the identity or fail owned-scope validation; include the helper and its tests in their own candidate coverage.
3. Validate repository-relative scope, exclusions and containment before any base/index/worktree content read. Reject escaping or absolute paths and directory-symlink ancestors; do not resolve an outside target and then read it to establish rejection. For an allowed final symlink, bind the link itself without opening its target. A scoped file matching an exclusion must be rejected or removed before byte access even if tracked and unchanged.
4. Exercise a boundary matrix before independent review: literal delimiter/whitespace names, actual rename records, nested untracked edits/additions, unexpected files, tracked and untracked exclusion overlaps, unchanged excluded files, directory symlinks, dangling links, outside targets, deletions and independent index/worktree/mode changes. Guard synthetic outside/excluded targets so a test fails on an attempted read, not merely on returned output.
5. Retain failing independent reproductions and bind new test receipts to the corrected identity. Do not describe an unaccepted helper as a proven manifest workflow; passing ordinary cases does not establish complete coverage or no-read exclusions.

Make long-running research and diagnostic work observable on the board: require a durable checkpoint after initial inspection, after each meaningful probe batch, and before an extended wait. Each checkpoint states evidence found, hypotheses eliminated, current action, blocker, and next decision; heartbeat/tool-call activity alone is not progress. Set an explicit update interval and execution budget at admission. Verify the installed native runtime setting and actual admission binding; a time limit written only in the task body is advisory. Report whether enforcement itself was exercised. Prefer a role allowance aligned with the existing supported native cap; do not turn ordinary local-work preflight into core watchdog testing or test-environment setup merely to strengthen an enforcement claim. At budget exhaustion, preserve partial evidence and return a bounded result or blocker instead of silently extending the investigation.

Do not mark an incident resolved merely because Orchestrator decomposed it. If an intake card must complete to release children, label that card and its completion as intake/decomposition, and retain a distinct final-acceptance card with the original acceptance criteria. Report research, remediation, delivery and production validation separately so board status cannot imply success prematurely. For production incidents, identify automatic deployment triggers before delivery: obtain scoped deployment authority before any push, merge, image publication or automation action that can deploy. Place reconciliation/rollout verification and production acceptance after the corresponding delivery/build evidence; a later deployment gate cannot protect an earlier auto-deploying publication. Verify deployed identity plus expected production row/file evidence before incident closure. The offline `close` gate checks repository closure only and does not enforce deployment or production acceptance. Never infer deployment permission from workflow recovery. See the contract's production-incident section.

When a user reports no progress, inspect the exact board, active attempt and worker activity before interrupting or redispatching; use [worker progress diagnosis and controlled restart](worker-progress.md). Do not infer a hang from an empty worker log or infer useful progress solely from a high tool count. When the user asks to stop and refactor, park the card against redispatch, separately verify the exact worker is idle, preserve its evidence, and revise the assignment before any restart. Reusing a card means a new attempt with retained history, not resuming the stopped execution. Keep live board mutations in the authorized canonical non-worker coordinator, or the parent as a disclosed temporary fallback. Delegate bounded preparation only; do not delegate the final transition to a child whose native mutation fence rejects it, because that creates another avoidable handoff. Preserve identity fences and read back every applied transition. Never announce a restart until the new attempt is observed. If the attempt crashes and the breaker parks it, diagnose the execution failure before another unblock; blocking after a failure is not itself the root cause.
